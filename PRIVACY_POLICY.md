# FitYar Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 22 October 2026**

FitYar is an offline workout-planning and workout-logging application.

## Information stored by the app

FitYar stores workout plans, exercise names, workout dates, sets, repetitions, weights, and step-counter values locally on your Android device. The app does not currently send this information to the developer or to a server.

The app does not currently include advertising, analytics, account registration, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Permissions

- **Physical activity (Android 10 and later):** requested only when you choose to use the step-counter feature. The app uses the device's step-counter sensor to display steps while that feature is active.
- **File creation:** when you choose to export your workout history, Android's document picker lets you choose where to save a CSV file. The app writes the export to the location you select.

## Sharing and retention

Your workout data remains in the app's local database until you delete it or uninstall the app. Exported CSV files are saved to a location you choose and are managed by you. Anyone with access to your device or exported files may be able to read that information.

## Children

FitYar is a general fitness utility and is not specifically designed for children.

## Changes to this policy

If future versions add advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, accounts, or other third-party services, this policy and the app's data disclosures must be updated before those features are released.
